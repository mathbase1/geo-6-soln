Find the area of the pink triangle

Every point is named, every angle is accounted for, and the final subtraction is shown pictorially.

Final answer 8(√3 − 1) square units ≈ 5.856 square units

The diagram gives AC = 8, AB = BD, a right angle at B, angle DAC = x, and angle ACB = 2x.

1Use the equal tick marks

The double tick on the vertical side and the double tick on the left part of the base mean those two lengths are equal.

AB=BD

Also, the square at B means

∠ABD=90°

Therefore triangle ABD is an isosceles right triangle. Because AB=BD, the angles opposite those equal sides are equal:

∠BAD=∠ADB

The two non-right angles together make 90°, so each one is

180°−90°2=45°
Key result: the angle between AB and AD is 45°.

Close-up of triangle ABD

Equal perpendicular legs force the other two angles to be 45° each.

2Solve for x

Now use the angle sum of the large triangle ABC.

The whole angle at A has two pieces:

∠BAC=∠BAD+∠DAC=45°+x

The angle at C is given as 2x, and the angle at B is 90°. Therefore:

90°+(45°+x)+2x=180°
135°+3x=180°
3x=45°
x=15°
So the angle marked 2x is 2×15°=30°.

Consequently the large triangle ABC has angles 90°, 60°, and 30°.

The three angles in the large triangle

At A, the 45° blue-triangle angle and the x angle sit next to each other, so the whole angle is 45° + x.

3Find AB from the 30° angle

Side AC is opposite the right angle, so AC is the hypotenuse. The diagram gives AC=8.

Relative to the 30° angle at C:

  • AB is the opposite side;
  • AC is the hypotenuse.
sin⁡30°=ABAC
12=AB8
AB=8×12=4
Key length: AB=4.
Why is sin 30° equal to 1/2?

It is the standard 30°–60°–90° triangle ratio. If an equilateral triangle of side 8 is cut in half, each half has hypotenuse 8 and a side of length 4 opposite the 30° angle. Hence opposite ÷ hypotenuse = 4 ÷ 8 = 1/2.

Use the large right triangle only

In a 30°–60°–90° triangle, the side opposite 30° is half the hypotenuse.

4Find BC, then use AB = BD

We now know two sides of the large right triangle: AC=8 and AB=4.

Apply Pythagoras to triangle ABC:

AB2+BC2=AC2
42+BC2=82
BC2=64−16=48
BC=48=43

From the matching tick marks in Step 1, BD=AB. Since AB=4,

BD=4
Lengths now known: AB=BD=4 and BC=43.

All lengths needed for the area

The point D splits the full base BC. Only the left piece BD is 4; the entire base BC is 4√3.

5Find the two areas we can calculate easily

The cleanest method is to calculate the area of the whole right triangle, then subtract the pale-blue right triangle.

Area of the large triangle ABC

Its perpendicular base and height are BC=43 and AB=4.

Area of ABC=12×43×4=83

Area of the blue triangle ABD

Its perpendicular legs are both 4.

Area of ABD=12×4×4=8
Do not subtract side lengths here. We are subtracting areas: the area of the complete triangle minus the area of its blue part.

The blue and pink regions do not overlap, and together they exactly fill the large triangle.

6Subtract to obtain the pink area

Triangle ACD is what remains after triangle ABD is removed from triangle ABC.

Pink area=area of ABC−area of ABD
Pink area=83−8
Pink area=8(3−1)
Exact answer: 8(3−1) square units.

As a decimal,

8(3−1)≈5.856

The required region only

Only triangle ACD remains coloured. Its exact area is 8(√3 − 1).

7Independent check using the pink triangle's own base

This is not needed for the solution, but it verifies that the answer is consistent.

The pink base is DC. Since BC=43 and BD=4,

DC=BC−BD=43−4

The perpendicular distance from A to the horizontal line through DC is still AB = 4. The foot of this perpendicular is B, which lies on the extension of DC to the left. That is allowed: a triangle's altitude may meet an extension of its base.

Area of ACD=12×DC×4
=12×(43−4)×4
=83−8=8(3−1)
✓ The direct base-height method gives exactly the same result.

Why AB can be used as the height

The altitude lands at B, outside the pink base segment DC, but it is still the perpendicular distance to the same straight line.

Final answer

The key chain of reasoning is:

  1. AB=BD makes triangle ABD a 45°–45°–90° triangle.
  2. The large triangle's angle sum gives x=15°, so the angle at C is 30°.
  3. With hypotenuse 8, AB=4 and BC=43.
  4. Pink area = whole area − blue area.
Size check: the whole triangle has area about 13.856 and the blue triangle has area 8, so the pink remainder should be about 5.856. That matches the exact result.
